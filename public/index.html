How It Works

1

Report a Missing Child

Fill out the form with all known details — name, age, address, last seen location, and a photo if available.

2

We Verify & Publish

Our admin team reviews every report to ensure accuracy, then publishes it so the community can help.

3

Community Helps Find

Anyone who sees the child can raise a found request. After verification, the family is reunited.
